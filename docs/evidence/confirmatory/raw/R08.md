In AI-led engineering projects, a PDCA task log is a short running record that notes what was planned, what was done, what was checked, and what should change next.
Because an AI can produce and revise work quickly, this log keeps a clear, shared history so humans can follow the reasoning, repeat good results, and fix mistakes instead of losing them in scattered chats.
For a newcomer, reading the loop of plan, do, check, and act makes the project easier to understand and shows why each small improvement was made.
